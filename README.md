# 6d-sugra-data-T01

All anomaly-free 6d SUGRA models with at most one tensor multiplet and gauge group containing no $\mathrm{U}(1)$, $\mathrm{SU}(2)$, $\mathrm{SU}(3)$ or $\mathrm{Sp}(2)$ factors.
Companion data to [[Hamada, GL : 2404.08845](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.08845)].

Models are organized by numbers of type $S$, $N$ and $T$ vertices and their data include a unique identifier, constituent vertices, anomaly lattice, and hypermultiplet spectrum.

Functionality for loading in, searching and analyzing models is provided in [analysis/helper.py](https://github.com/gloges/6d-sugra-data-T01/blob/efcc97af55408db7634f7eb5828610ab418febfd/analysis/helper.py) and exemplified in the iPython notebook [analysis/example.ipynb](https://github.com/gloges/6d-sugra-data-T01/blob/efcc97af55408db7634f7eb5828610ab418febfd/analysis/example.ipynb).
